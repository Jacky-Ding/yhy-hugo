Suspension failures are rarely sudden breakages — the overwhelming majority are **accumulated assembly and maintenance errors**.

## Leaf springs

The fracture location tells you the cause. **Break at the centre bolt hole** — loose U-bolts, stress concentrated. **Broken leaf ends** — no inter-leaf lubrication, dry friction thins the tips. **Sagging pack** — chronic overload plus insufficient material hardness.

We build to SHACMAN's original leaf count and camber. For mine or mountain duty customers can add leaves, at the cost of a harsher unladen ride — worth stating up front, or they will complain the truck "bounces" after fitting.

## Balance shaft

Assembly, housing, bushes. Worn bushes show as rear axle oscillation and uneven tyre wear. Many fleets replace only the bush — but if the journal is already grooved, the new bush will not last three months.

## U-bolts

U-bolt specification and torque differ by model and axle type — give us the model and we ship the matching set. Low unit price, but a missing or under-torqued U-bolt breaks leaf springs directly — the classic small part that triggers a major repair.
