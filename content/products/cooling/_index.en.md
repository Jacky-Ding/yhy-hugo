Thermal management is the first thing to fail on a heavy truck in tropical markets. In Zambia and Tanzania the dry-season ground temperature is high and the cooling system's margin is thin, so any loss of efficiency shows up immediately on the gauge.

## Three levels

**Radiator** — core, seals, tanks. Aluminium cores are light and efficient but sensitive to coolant quality; copper is durable and easier to repair. We recommend aluminium for export, with a clear reminder to use compliant coolant.

**Intercooler** — lowers charge temperature and raises density. A blocked or leaking intercooler shows as **lost power, not high coolant temperature**, which is frequently misdiagnosed as a turbo problem.

**Water pump and pulleys** — the warning signs are seepage and noise; by the time a pulley lets go it has usually taken the fan or radiator with it.

## Extra advice for desert duty

A bug screen in front of the radiator and regular blow-down are more effective than buying more expensive parts. We include a short maintenance note with shipments; customers tell us this small detail is genuinely useful.
