"Cranks slowly" and "does not crank at all" are two completely different fault directions — guessing wrong means fitting parts that were never needed.

## Symptom mapping

**Nothing at all, no click** — check the starter relay, ignition switch and neutral switch first; usually not the motor itself.

**Clicks but will not crank** — low battery, poor earth, or burnt solenoid contacts. Measure battery voltage during cranking: **below 18 V means the battery, not the starter**.

**Cranks slowly and weakly** — worn brushes or a shorted armature inside the motor. This is the case that justifies replacement.

**Cranks but the engine will not fire** — slipping pinion or a failed one-way clutch; replace the motor, and inspect the flywheel ring gear at the same time.

## Power rating

5.5 kW is the standard fitment; 6.5 kW suits **high-compression, large-displacement or cold-climate** starting. For customers complaining about winter starting, upgrading to 6.5 kW works better than repeatedly replacing batteries.

## Supply and warranty

Reduction type is the default (compact, high torque); direct-drive also available. Pinions come in 10T and 11T — **confirm the tooth count when ordering**, because the wrong pinion destroys the flywheel ring gear and costs far more than the starter.

No-load and load tested at the factory. **Warranty 6 months**.
