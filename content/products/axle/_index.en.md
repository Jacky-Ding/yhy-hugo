Front axle and drive axle parts are high-value, long-interval items, and customers scrutinise them far more than filters — which makes **part number accuracy more important than price**.

## Front axle

Sub-blocks: **steering tie rods**, **brake group** (backing plate, shoes, camshaft), **king pin repair kits** (pin, bushings, seals), **housing** (body, steering knuckle).

King pin kits move fastest. A worn king pin shows up as vague steering and feathered tyre wear — many fleets replace two tyres before realising the pin was the problem.

## Drive axle

Sub-blocks: **final drive** (crown wheel and pinion), **differential** (with lock), **wheel-end reduction**, **half shafts**, **attachments**.

Ratio is the critical parameter: 4.11 for linehaul, 4.625 general purpose, 5.286 for heavy tipper and mine work. Always state the ratio — a wrong part costs more in return freight than the part itself.

## Wheels

Hubs, wheel ends and tyre specification. The catalogue includes a tyre fundamentals section covering ply rating, tread pattern and pressure matching.
