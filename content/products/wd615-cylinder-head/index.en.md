The WD615 is the highest-volume heavy-duty diesel platform built on Steyr technology, powering SHACMAN Delong, CNHTC HOWO, FAW J5/J6 and a large share of tipper trucks still in service across Africa, Russia and Southeast Asia.

## When the head actually needs replacing

Three failure modes account for most of the demand. **Cracking between valve seats** — the classic "nose bridge" crack, showing up as persistent coolant loss and bubbles in the expansion tank. **Distortion after overheating** — flatness goes out of tolerance, the gasket stops sealing and compression escapes into the adjacent cylinder. **Core plug and oil gallery seepage** — no visible leak, but the engine keeps losing coolant.

The first two are replacement cases. Welding repairs rarely survive heavy-duty duty cycles.

## Bare head or complete assembly

We supply both. The **bare head** suits workshops that already hold valve train components and want the lowest landed cost. The **complete assembly** arrives with valves, springs, seals and camshaft bushes fitted — ready to bolt on, which is what most fleet operators order.

Every unit is pressure-tested and checked for flatness before dispatch; assemblies are additionally checked for valve recession.

## Fit it once, properly

Replace the head gasket and head bolts at the same time. WD615 head bolts are torque-to-yield and single-use — reusing them is the single most common cause of a second failure three months later.
