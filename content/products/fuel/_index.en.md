A fuel tank looks like the least technical part on the truck, but three issues come up constantly in African markets.

## Aluminium tanks

SHACMAN mainly fits aluminium tanks from 400 L to 1000 L. **Capacity and mounting hole spacing must match together** — the same model with different options can have different bracket spacing.

Aluminium will not rust, but it **wears badly**: once the bracket rubbers perish, tank and bracket grind directly and can wear through in months. We therefore ship new rubbers with every tank by default. Customers are often puzzled at first and understand after one season.

## Level sensors

The usual failure is **jumping readings**, not total failure. A sticking float or worn track makes the gauge swing in the mid-range, so the driver stops trusting mileage estimates — a real operational cost.

## Caps and straps

Caps come locking and non-locking, steel and plastic. For markets with fuel theft, specify locking caps. Broken straps let the tank shift and are a common cause of breakdowns, yet cost almost nothing — the kind of item worth keeping a batch of.
