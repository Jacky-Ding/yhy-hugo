Intake and exhaust parts fail in a predictable line: **noise first, then leaks, then lost power**. Customers usually wait until power drops noticeably, by which point the turbo may already be affected.

## Exhaust side

Pipes, silencers, flexible bellows, clamps. The bellows cracks first — it absorbs vibration and thermal expansion, and once split, back pressure drops, noise rises, and hot gas can cook nearby wiring and air lines.

Silencers rust through quickly in high-salinity coastal air; customers in Africa and the Middle East report noticeably different service life.

## Intake side

Air cleaner assemblies and elements. In dusty conditions — mine sites, unpaved roads — element life can be half of what it is domestically, making this one of the steadiest repeat items for African customers.

## Urea system

Euro IV and above models run SCR, with the urea tank and associated lines in this block. Where local urea supply does not exist, some customers delete the system; we do not perform such modifications, but will supply genuine part numbers so they can decide for themselves.
