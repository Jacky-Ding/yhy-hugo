Leaf springs look simple, but material heat treatment and inter-leaf friction control decide how long they actually last.

## Breakage is rarely bad luck

- **Fracture at the centre bolt hole** — U-bolts worked loose, stress concentrated; insufficient tightening torque is the usual root cause
- **Broken leaf ends** — dry friction between leaves thins the tips when lubrication is absent
- **Sagging pack (lost camber)** — overload plus under-hardened material; poor tempering control produces exactly this
- **Cascading failures** — one broken leaf left in service overloads the rest

## How we control the spec

60Si2Mn, quenched and tempered to HRC 42–48. Too soft and the pack sags; too hard and it snaps. This band is the industry-proven balance.

Leaves carry a graphite coating to cut dry friction — a difference you notice immediately in dusty African conditions.

## Custom builds

Customers routinely add or remove leaves for local road conditions. Give us the vehicle load, typical terrain and original leaf count, and we will propose a leaf count and camber. **MOQ is 10 pieces** — heat treatment runs as a full batch, so smaller quantities are not economic.
