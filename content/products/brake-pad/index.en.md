Brake pads move fastest in heavy truck aftermarket and are also where quality differences become obvious to the customer.

## Where cheap pads cut corners

Low-cost pads usually economise in three places: **excess resin** (cheap, but crumbles and fades under heat), **insufficient steel fibre** (unstable friction coefficient), and **untreated backing plates** (seize in the caliper within months in humid climates).

The result: braking feels fine at first, then fades badly after 20,000 km. On a loaded descent the driver blames the master cylinder.

## Where our compound sits

Non-asbestos semi-metallic, FF grade (0.38–0.42), rated −40 °C to 650 °C.

Not the longest-wearing and not the quietest — but stable under **heavy descent, tropical heat and dust**, which is exactly the duty profile of our main markets in Africa, the Middle East and Southeast Asia.

## Supply

Both drum and disc applications, covering the BPW, SAF and Fuwa axle families plus OEM SHACMAN Delong and CNHTC HOWO axles. **MOQ 50 sets**, mixed specifications can be combined onto one pallet.

Backing plates are phosphated for corrosion resistance — no greasing required for sea freight.
