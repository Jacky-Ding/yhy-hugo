The turbo is one of the highest-failure accessories on the WD615, but a large share of "bad turbo" diagnoses turn out to be something else.

## Rule it out first

- **Persistent blue smoke with oil in the intercooler** — seals have failed, replacement justified
- **Low boost but the shaft spins freely** — usually a leaking intercooler, delaminated charge hose or a blown exhaust manifold gasket
- **Hissing under hard acceleration** — check the intercooler core and clamps before condemning the turbo
- **Grinding and wheel contact** — bearing failure, replace it, but also find out what was ingested

These three checks prevent a meaningful share of unnecessary replacements.

## What we supply

HX35W, HX40W and equivalents covering the mainstream WD615 configurations. Each unit is dynamically balanced and leak-tested, gaskets included.

**Exhaust manifold, intercooler and oil feed pipe are not included** — they fail independently, and bundling them muddies the quotation.

## Fitting matters more than the part

Three things are non-negotiable: fresh oil and filter, a cleaned oil feed line, and manual pre-lube of the bearing housing. **Most early failures trace back to a dry start**, not to the turbo itself.
