Cab parts have the **widest price spread** in export: from a few dollars for an oil seal to four figures for a complete cab assembly.

## Lighting

Headlight assemblies, combination tail lights, turn signals, side markers, position lights. Connectors differ by model year — X3000 and F3000 headlights do not interchange, which is the single most common ordering error in this category.

Lighting failure rates are high in Africa, and not purely a quality issue: **unstable voltage** from overcharging or tired batteries burns bulbs repeatedly. When a customer claims bulbs repeatedly, check the regulator first.

## Interior and controls

Instrument clusters, tachometers, steering wheels, mirrors, seat belts. Cluster failures are usually **dead needles or failed backlighting**; replacement is more economical than repair.

## Exterior

Bumpers, front mask, fenders, steps, windscreens. Windscreens ship in purpose-built timber racks — breakage rates track packaging quality directly, so we build upright crates to OEM standard.

## Air conditioning

Cab A/C is essential in tropical markets; the compressor and condenser are the main failure points.
