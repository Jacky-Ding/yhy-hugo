Powertrain parts share one trait: the warning signs are obvious long before failure, but customers routinely wait until it stops working. By the time the gearbox refuses to engage, the cover or flywheel is usually damaged too.

## Three blocks

**Clutch** — cover, driven disc, release bearing, slave cylinder. On mine-site trucks with frequent loaded starts, disc life can be as low as 80,000 km.

**Transmission** — SHACMAN mainly fits Fast 9-, 12- and 16-speed twin-countershaft boxes. Shift mechanism, selector mechanism and range-box synchronizers are the wear points.

**Driveshaft** — assemblies, universal joints, centre bearings. Dry universal joints are the leading cause of shaft failure; many fleets only remember to grease them after a shaft lets go.

## Sizing note

Clutch cover part numbers are tied to the **engine torque rating**. The same truck model with a different power rating takes a different cover. Give us the engine model from the vehicle plate and we will match the correct cover part number directly.
