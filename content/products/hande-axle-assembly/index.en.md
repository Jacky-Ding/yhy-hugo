Hande axles are fitted to a very large share of Chinese heavy trucks; export demand concentrates on the aftermarket in Africa and the Middle East.

## Choosing the ratio

The ratio sets the relationship between engine speed and road speed — get it wrong and you pay in fuel or in gradeability:

- **4.11** — linehaul tractors, high economic cruise speed, suited to flat long-haul routes
- **4.625** — general purpose, workable for both tractor and tipper duty
- **5.286** — heavy tippers, mining and mountainous work; strong climbing, low top speed

A customer running only local delivery who buys 4.11 will report "no power on hills"; one running a mine site who buys 5.286 will complain about fuel on the highway. **Confirming the duty cycle matters more than confirming the part number.**

## Supply form

- **Complete axle** — housing, final drive, wheel ends, brakes; bolt-on
- **Final drive assembly** — where the housing is still serviceable
- **Component parts** — crown wheel and pinion, differential, half shafts

## Export packing

Complete axles ship in purpose-built timber cradles, with brake chambers and oil ports blanked. We apply rust preventive and provide a packing list for customs clearance.
