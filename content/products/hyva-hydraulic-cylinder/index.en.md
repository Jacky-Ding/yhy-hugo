The front-end lifting cylinder is the most expensive single item in a tipper's hydraulic system, and the easiest to misdiagnose.

## Is it the cylinder, or the system?

- **Weak lift but it does rise slowly** — check the pump and relief valve first, not the cylinder
- **Stops halfway** — usually the limit valve or air in the line
- **Lifts fine but will not come down** — sticking lowering valve, not internal leakage
- **External seepage on the barrel** — seal failure, rebuild kit required
- **Noise and judder while lifting** — scored bore or ingested air

**Only external seepage, and internal leakage confirmed with a healthy pump, actually indict the cylinder.** Misdiagnose and even a new cylinder will not fix it.

## Supply options

- **Complete cylinder** — barrel, rod and seals; for a scored bore
- **Seal repair kit** — about 15–20% of a complete cylinder, for internal leakage on a sound bore
- **Rod only** — for chrome flaking or a bent rod

## About the seals

Imported polyurethane seal sets rated above 25 MPa. Cheap seals extrude and deform under pressure and typically last less than half as long.
